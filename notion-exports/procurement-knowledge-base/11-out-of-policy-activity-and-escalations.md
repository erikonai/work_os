# april Out-of-Policy Activity & Escalations

This page pulls together what counts as out-of-policy spend across cards, invoices, travel, and the work from home stipend, and what happens next.

## What counts as out-of-policy

### Corporate card

- Failing to upload receipts or add a memo within 7 days of the transaction
- Expensing non-allowable charges, including personal expenses on a corporate card, and business expenses not approved under the april Spend Policy, not approved by a manager, or not covered by the relevant budget

### Invoices

- Processing invoices without proper approval
- Missing or incomplete supporting documentation
- Late submission of approvals that delays payment

### Travel

- Booking travel on any platform other than Ramp, which is not reimbursed
- Booking travel less than 1 week in advance
- Booking above the 15% above-market price limit without approval from both your direct manager and HR. Ramp marks these **"Out of Policy"** in red

### Work from home stipend

- Buying items outside the approved list, or at a vendor other than Amazon, Apple, or Dell

## How activity gets caught

| **Area** | **Who reviews** | **Cadence** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate card spend | Finance and the People Team | Every two weeks |
| Invoice processing | Finance | Ongoing monitoring |
| Work from home stipend purchases | Finance | Subject to audit at any time |

## What happens next

> ⚠️ **Any out-of-policy card activity**
> - Finance and the People Team notify your card manager

> ⛔ **Out-of-policy card activity above $100**
> - You are asked to reimburse april for the amount

> 🚨 **Three out-of-policy events**
> - Your spending privileges are revoked
> - All your active company corporate cards are suspended

> ⚠️ **Repeated invoice violations**
> - Escalated to department leadership for review

> ⛔ **Non-compliant stipend purchase**
> - You, your manager, and the People Team are notified
> - You reimburse april for the full amount through Ramp
> - If you do not comply, the amount may be deducted from future payments

## If you catch it yourself

> 🚨 If you put a personal charge on a corporate card by accident, you **MUST** contact Finance and the People Team immediately. The same goes for returning something you were already reimbursed for.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to the Bills Policy are approved by both Finance and department leadership. Exceptions on cards, stipends, and travel go to the People Team or Finance, before the purchase rather than after.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - Something you think is out of policy: Finance and the People Team, before the next review.
> - Disputing a flagged transaction: Finance.
> - Requesting an exception: your manager, then Finance.
