# april Travel Expenses, Per Diem & Safety

This page covers what happens once you are on the trip. For approving and booking the trip, see Business Travel Booking.

## Per diem

You receive a daily per diem of $150 during approved business travel, for meals and incidental expenses. The amount is loaded automatically onto your physical Ramp card for each approved travel day. It covers:

- Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner
- Coffee and snacks
- Rideshare within the destination city
- Tips for meals and services
- Other small daily expenses

> ⚠️ Transportation to and from the airport is not part of the per diem. Expense those trips separately in Ramp. No prior approval needed.

## Receipts

> 🚨 A receipt **IS REQUIRED** for any expense above $75.

For expenses under $75, Ramp generates a digital receipt automatically. Nothing else is needed.

## Expenses outside Ramp

All business expenses are tracked in Ramp automatically. In the rare case an expense happens outside Ramp:

1. Submit the receipt within 7 business days.
2. Include a clear business purpose.
3. Confirm the expense complies with company policy, the same as any Ramp expense.

## What april does not pay for

> 🚨 These **ARE NOT** reimbursable:
> - Personal entertainment
> - Spouse and family travel expenses
> - Flight upgrades

## Travel safety

For your safety and security:

- Register international travel with the People Team
- Keep your emergency contact information updated
- Save copies of important documents
- Follow local health and safety guidelines

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - Per diem not loaded, or a Ramp card problem on the road: Finance.
> - An expense that happened outside Ramp: Finance.
> - International travel registration and emergencies: the People Team.
