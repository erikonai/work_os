# TL;DR

> ⭐ A Spend Request is how you ask april to buy something, and it is the only purchase-request workflow we run. File one in Ramp for any purchase over $500, and for every software subscription, professional service, contractor, consultant, license, or piece of IT infrastructure at any price. # april Spend Requests

This page covers when you need to file a Spend Request and what information you need in hand. For who approves it and how long each step takes, see Purchase Approval Process & Timelines.

## When a Spend Request is required

> 🚨 A Spend Request **MUST** be approved before you commit april to the purchase. Do not sign an order form, accept terms, or start a trial that converts to paid until approval is complete.

Submit a Spend Request for:

- Any purchase over $500
- All Software/SaaS subscriptions regardless of cost, including renewals of existing subscriptions
- All Professional Services engagements
- All Contractor agreements
- All Consulting Services
- All Licenses and Permits
- All IT Infrastructure purchases

Purchases under $500 that fall outside those categories go on your corporate card. See Corporate Card Usage & Guidelines.

## Express path for low-risk purchases

> 🚧 This path is agreed in design but **NOT YET LIVE** in Ramp. Until it is switched on, every Spend Request runs the full chain in Purchase Approval Process & Timelines. Do not plan around a faster turnaround yet.

Some purchases give Security nothing to assess: catering, branded merchandise, one-off supplies bought on a vendor's checkout page. Those will run a shorter chain of your manager, then Finance, then a light Legal check of the vendor's standard terms, then the PO issues. Four steps instead of six.

A purchase takes the express path when all five of these are true. You do not choose it, and you cannot opt in. Ramp works it out from your answers.

- The vendor handles no april data beyond anonymized or aggregated, and gets no access to april systems, networks, or data
- The vendor does not move funds and is not on the product critical path
- It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, auto-renewal, or multi-year term
- The total is $10,000 or less
- The vendor does not require april to sign an agreement. A checkout page, quote, or invoice is fine. An MSA or SOW is not.

> ℹ️ **Access means access to systems, networks, or data.** Someone being physically on site is not access on its own. A caterer delivering lunch does not fail this test. A vendor pulling cable or working in the IT room does, because that is access to the network.

Book catering under **Events**, not Facilities/Maintenance. Facilities/Maintenance is for work on the premises themselves, which is far more likely to involve on-site infrastructure and should not reach the express path.

> 🚨 If any one of those five is false, the request **RUNS THE STANDARD CHAIN**. Finance can also move any request back to the standard chain at any point.

An express request never asks you for NDA status. A one-time purchase on the vendor's own paper, with no data and no system access, needs no NDA, so the question is answered for you.

Legal signs off on every express purchase. It is a general sign-off rather than a contract review, since a negotiated agreement disqualifies a purchase from this path, so Legal is reading the vendor's standard online terms at a 3 day target rather than the 7 days a negotiated contract takes. Every purchase that reaches a purchase order has had legal eyes on it, whichever path it took.

## NDA status

The request asks you for NDA status. Your answer decides how Legal runs the first approval step, so pick the option that matches where the vendor actually stands.

| **If you select** | **Legal does this** |
| --- | --- |
| The vendor will sign April's standard NDA | Sends april's NDA to your vendor contact and uploads the executed copy |
| The vendor prefers that we review and sign their NDA | Reviews and signs the vendor-provided NDA, then uploads the executed copy |
| Vendor already has a signed NDA | Reviews and approves the existing NDA and uploads it |
| NDA is not needed | Confirms no NDA is required for this engagement |

Unless you select "NDA is not needed", an executed NDA **MUST** be uploaded before the request moves past Legal. Legal targets 3 days on this step.

## The questions you will be asked

The form asks eleven required questions and two optional ones. Have these answers ready before you start.

> 🚧 The question set below is what the form asks **TODAY**. A revised set is agreed in design and not yet built. It adds questions about what april data the vendor touches, what access it needs, whether it moves funds or sits on the product critical path, and whether it requires a signed agreement. Those answers will decide your approval path, so an express purchase will stop after the first eleven questions and a standard one will continue to nineteen.

| **Question** | **What to enter** |
| --- | --- |
| What entity is this for? | April Tax Solutions Inc., April Tax Solutions Ltd., or May Advisory LLC |
| What type of purchase is this? | One of eleven categories (see below). This is what routes your request to Security, HR, or neither. Book catering under Events. |
| Who is the selected vendor for this purchase? | The official business name the vendor uses on contracts and invoices. Use "Salesforce, Inc." rather than "Salesforce", and "ABC Consulting LLC" rather than "John's consulting business". |
| What department code should this be charged to? | The department whose budget absorbs the cost. Ask your manager or Finance if you are unsure. |
| Is there current available budget? | Yes or No |
| What is the primary business need this addresses? | The specific problem this solves or opportunity it captures. Clear justification moves faster. |
| When do you need this by? | A date. Leave room for approvals, vendor setup, and implementation. |
| What is the urgency level of this request? | Priority 1, 2, or 3 (see below) |
| What are the requested payment terms? | The number of days allowed for payment after invoice receipt. Enter 30 for Net 30. |
| What is the vendor's address? | The vendor's mailing address for official correspondence, which is not always their billing address. |
| NDA Status | One of the four options in the section above |
| Are there any special instructions or notes for the PO? *(optional)* | Delivery instructions, reference numbers, or anything else that belongs on the purchase order |
| Any supporting documents? *(optional)* | Quotes, order forms, contracts, or anything showing pricing |

## Line items

> 🚨 Every request **MUST** carry at least one line item before it can be submitted. A request with no line items will not go through.

Each line item takes a description, quantity, unit price, and the start and end dates it covers. Break out what you are actually buying rather than entering a single lump sum, since Legal and Finance review the line items against the vendor's order form.

## Paying by virtual card

The form asks whether you want to pay with a virtual card. If you say yes, the card is issued only after the request is fully approved, never before. This is a payment method on your Spend Request, not a separate request type.

## After you submit

Track the request in Ramp to see its current stage and who it is waiting on. An approved request ends with a purchase order issued to the vendor. If Legal, Security, or HR asks for more detail, the request waits until you answer.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - Whether a purchase needs a Spend Request: your manager first, then Finance.
> - NDA questions, or a vendor that will not sign: Legal.
> - Vendor security and data questions: Security.
> - Ramp access or a stuck request: Finance.
