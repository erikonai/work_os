# april Expense Reimbursements

On rare occasions you may pay for a company expense yourself, usually a cash purchase or a business purchase charged to a personal card by mistake. Any necessary, reasonable, and actually incurred expense you paid for as part of authorized company business can be submitted for reimbursement. Travel per diem is handled separately, on Travel Expenses, Per Diem & Safety.

> 🚨 Reimbursements **MUST** be submitted through Ramp, and no later than 5 business days after month-end.

> 🚨 Expenses submitted more than 45 days after the transaction date **WILL NOT** be approved for reimbursement.

## What to include

- Date of the transaction
- Merchant name
- Amount to be reimbursed
- Detailed description of the transaction

## Who reviews it

Every reimbursement request is reviewed by your manager, the Finance Team, and the People Team, and approved from there.

## When you get paid

| **When you submit** | **When you are paid** |
| --- | --- |
| On time: within 5 business days after month-end | No later than 15 business days after that same month-end |
| Late: more than 5 business days after month-end | Within the first two weeks of the following month |

If you have a valid need for an expedited reimbursement, reach out to Finance and the People Team.

## Returns

> 🚨 If you return a purchase that was already reimbursed, you **MUST** notify Finance and the People Team so the reimbursement can be reversed.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - Whether an expense qualifies: your manager, then Finance.
> - An expedited reimbursement: Finance and the People Team.
> - A rejected or stuck Ramp submission: Finance.
