# april Business Spend Overview

This page covers what april spends money on, how to judge whether a purchase is worth making, and what we look at before we add a vendor.

## What business spend covers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ Office Supplies & Equipment** | Essential items needed for daily operations and workspace functionality |
| **2️⃣ Software & Subscriptions** | Tools and services required for business operations |
| **3️⃣ Professional Services** | Consulting, legal, or other specialized business services |
| **4️⃣ Marketing & Advertising** | Promotional materials, campaigns, and advertising costs |
| **5️⃣ Training & Development** | Professional development courses, certifications, and learning resources |
| **6️⃣ Team Events** | Approved team building activities and company events |

## How we decide

> 🚨 Business spend **MUST** be discussed with your manager before you commit april to it.

We optimize between practicality and cost, and we want the team to have what it needs to do good work. Before you buy, ask three questions:

1. Is this expense necessary for our success?
2. Are there more cost-effective alternatives?
3. Will this purchase meaningfully improve our work or team experience?

Every dollar we save can be reinvested in growth and in our people.

## What we look at before adding a vendor

> 1️⃣ **Business Value**
> - Validate purchases address business challenges
> - Ensure investments are necessary and timely
> - Quantify business impact
> - Align with company strategy

> 2️⃣ **Vendor Risk Management**
> - Partner with stable, reputable vendors
> - Verify track record through references
> - Confirm security standards
> - Ensure data protection

> 3️⃣ **Company Protection**
> - Secure favorable contract terms
> - Maintain exit options
> - Meet compliance requirements
> - Establish clear performance standards

> 🚨 Every new vendor **MUST** complete the Vendor Security & Risk Questionnaire and be assigned a risk tier before onboarding. See the Vendor Risk & Security Requirements page for tiers, evidence, and reassessment.
