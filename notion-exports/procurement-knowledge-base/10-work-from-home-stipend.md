# april Work From Home Stipend

april provides a one-time Work From Home stipend to eligible US employees to help you set up a productive and comfortable workspace. The stipend is distributed through Ramp and used within 12 months of your start date.

## How much you get

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ 🕒 Temporary Teams** | $250 |
| **2️⃣ 🧰 Business Teams** | $500 |
| **3️⃣ 💡 Builder Teams** | $750 |

Roles are grouped into three categories:

- **🕒 Temporary** covers temporary roles in our Tax Operations team.
- **🧰 Business Teams** are the teams that support how we run and grow the business: Tax Operations, Finance, Legal, People, Marketing, Sales, and Partnerships.
- **💡 Builder Teams** are the teams that build and evolve the product: Engineering, Product Management, Product Design, Data Science, and Tax Logic.

## Approved vendors

> 🚨 Your stipend **MUST** be spent at Amazon, Apple, or Dell. Your Ramp card is vendor-restricted, and no other vendor or reimbursement is permitted unless Finance pre-approves it.

## What it covers

> ✅ **What you can use it for**
>
> Work-related equipment and accessories that improve your setup. For example:
> - External monitors, keyboards, mice
> - Laptop stands, docks, webcams, microphones, headphones
> - Ergonomic chairs, desks, desk lighting
> - Adapters, cables, power accessories

> 🚫 **What is not covered**
>
> Personal, non-work, ongoing, or subscription expenses. For example:
> - Home internet or personal mobile plans
> - General-purpose electronics such as TVs and speakers
> - Furniture unrelated to your workspace
> - Gift cards, resale items, or other forms of reimbursement

## Using your Ramp card

Request access to the spend program, and you receive a Ramp card preloaded with your stipend amount and restricted to the approved vendors. To stay in policy:

- Use it only at Amazon, Apple, or Dell
- Upload a receipt in Ramp for each purchase

## Timeframe

> 🚨 You have 12 months from your start date to use the stipend. After that, any unused amount **EXPIRES** and cannot be reissued or carried over.

If you leave april, or your employment ends for any reason, any unused stipend is forfeited and is not paid out in cash or other compensation.

## Purchase auditing and compliance

Every stipend purchase is subject to audit by Finance. If a purchase falls outside the approved items list or otherwise breaks this policy:

1. You, your manager, and the People Team are notified of the non-compliant purchase.
2. You are required to reimburse april for the full amount through Ramp.
3. If you do not comply with the reimbursement request, the amount may be deducted from future payments.

april reserves the right to review and request documentation for any purchase made with the stipend.

## Equipment return

> ✅ Employees **are not required** to return equipment purchased with the WFH stipend or provided by the company. The equipment is yours to keep, even if you leave april.

If you have questions about what needs to be returned in your specific situation, contact the People Team.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - A different vendor, or a special case such as an accessibility need: the People Team or Finance, before you buy.
> - Card not loaded or a declined purchase: Finance.
> - Which stipend tier applies to your role: the People Team.
