# april Corporate Card Usage & Guidelines

Corporate cards are issued to all full-time employees to cover business purchases and travel. Eligibility starts on your first day of full-time employment.

## What you can use the card for

Subject to your individual card limit and prior manager approval where required:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ Monthly Stipends** | Approved monthly stipends such as wellness, learning, and home office |
| **2️⃣ Travel & Transportation** | Approved business travel, accommodations, and local transport |
| **3️⃣ Meals & Entertainment** | Approved business meals and client entertainment within policy limits |

## The rules

> 🚨 Your corporate card **SHOULD** be used for all company expenses. Do not put company purchases on a personal card and claim them back.

> 🚨 Corporate cards **MUST NOT** be used for personal expenses. If it happens by accident, contact Finance and the People Team immediately.

> 🚨 Receipts and memos **MUST** be uploaded within 7 days of the transaction. Missing either one counts as out-of-policy activity.

- **Departmental charges.** Keep charges limited to expenses within your own department wherever you can.
- **Card requests.** Use the appropriate spend program when you request a new card. For an ad-hoc request, give the amount, the intended use, and the business rationale.

## What the card does not cover

Purchases over $500, and any software, professional service, contractor, consultant, license, or IT infrastructure at any price, go through a Spend Request first. See Requesting a Purchase: Spend Requests.

## Out-of-policy card spend

Finance and the People Team review card spend every two weeks. Three out-of-policy events revoke your spending privileges and suspend your active cards. See Out-of-Policy Activity & Escalations for the full detail.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - Before making a purchase you are unsure about: the People Team or Finance.
> - A personal charge on the card by mistake: Finance and the People Team, immediately.
> - A lost card, a declined transaction, or a limit increase: Finance.
