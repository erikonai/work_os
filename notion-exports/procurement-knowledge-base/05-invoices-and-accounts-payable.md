# april Invoices & Accounts Payable

This page covers how a vendor invoice gets paid. For approving a purchase before it happens, see Purchase Approval Process & Timelines.

## Submitting an invoice

> 🚨 Send all invoices and receipts to [ap@getapril.com](mailto:ap@getapril.com). Every invoice **MUST** be processed through Ramp. Invoices routed through any other channel are not accepted.

Each invoice processed through Ramp carries complete vendor information, payment terms, and detailed line items for all charges.

### Required invoice information

- Vendor name and contact information
- Invoice number and date
- Purchase Order number, if applicable
- Department code
- Itemized description of goods or services
- Total amount due

If any required information is missing, the invoice goes back to the vendor for completion before we process it.

## Approval rules

- **Approval requirements.** Every invoice is approved by either the original requestor or the Department Owner, confirming that the goods or services were received.
- **Purchase order matching.** An invoice without a matching purchase order needs explicit Department Owner approval.
- **Documentation standards.** Complete supporting documentation is required, including proof of delivery or service completion.
- **Timeline.** Approvers review invoices within 3 business days of receipt.

> 🚨 Approvers **MUST** review invoices within 3 business days of receipt. Late approvals delay payment to the vendor.

> 🚨 Department Owners have authority to approve invoices within their budget and department scope. Anything outside that scope **REQUIRES** additional Finance team approval.

## How an invoice moves

1. The Accounting team receives the invoice.
2. Accounting reviews it for completeness and routes it to the right approver.
3. The approver reviews and either approves or disputes the charges.
4. Once approved, the invoice is processed for payment according to terms.

## Invoice approval authority by amount

This table governs who signs off on an invoice once the purchase itself is already approved. It does not govern purchase approval, which follows the Spend Request workflow in Ramp.

| **Annual spend range** | **Required approvals** |
| --- | --- |
| $0 - $499 | Direct Manager |
| $500 - $24,999 | Direct Manager + Finance |
| $25,000 - $99,999 | Direct Manager + Department Head + Finance |
| $100,000+ | Direct Manager + Department Head + Finance + Executive |

## Exceptions

Any exception to this policy is approved by both Finance and department leadership. For what counts as an invoice violation and what follows, see Out-of-Policy Activity & Escalations.

> 🆘 **Where to get help**
> - An invoice you cannot approve or do not recognize: Accounting.
> - A vendor asking about payment status: Accounting.
> - Exceptions: Finance and your department leadership.
