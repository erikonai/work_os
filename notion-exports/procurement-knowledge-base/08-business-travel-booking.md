# TL;DR

> ⭐ Business travel is approved by your manager and booked in Ramp, at least 1 week ahead. Economy is always allowed, Economy Plus on flights of 3 hours or more, and Premium Economy or Business on flights of 5 hours or more. Flights and hotels can run up to 15% above market rate. Going past that needs your manager and HR to approve before you book.

# april Business Travel Booking

Travel is approved when it is necessary, through discussion with your manager. These are the situations where travel is normally justified.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **1️⃣ Complex tasks that cannot be effectively completed remotely** | When the work directly aligns with key corporate objectives and strategy |
| **2️⃣ Performance reviews** | Managers may conduct quarterly or annual reviews in person |
| **3️⃣ Strategic events** | Conferences or meetups with significant business or revenue potential |
| **4️⃣ Client engagement** | Meeting key clients or prospects to demonstrate april products and features |
| **5️⃣ Team collaboration** | Essential in-person meetings that drive business objectives |
| **6️⃣ Training & onboarding** | Critical in-person sessions for new hires or role transitions needing hands-on instruction |

## Booking rules

> 🚨 All travel **MUST** be booked through Ramp. No exceptions. Travel booked on another platform will not be reimbursed.

> 🚨 All travel **MUST** be booked at least 1 week in advance through Ramp. No exceptions.

## Flight class

| **Flight length** | **Highest class allowed** |
| --- | --- |
| Any length | Economy |
| 3 hours or longer | Economy Plus |
| 5 hours or longer | Premium Economy or Business |

## Price limits

Flights and hotels may be booked up to 15% above market rate. Market rate is the average price at the time of purchase.

> 🚨 A booking above the 15% limit **REQUIRES** explicit approval from both your direct manager and HR before you book. Ramp marks these **"Out of Policy"** in red.
